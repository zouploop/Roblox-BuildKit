# Civic street furniture — build spec

## Shared style target

- Every preset is one independently placeable `kind: "prop"` raw-part build centered at local X/Z = 0.
- Ground contact is Y = 0 with no part below Y = -0.05; the footprint is at least 0.9× the widest elevated mass for a stable silhouette.
- Palette is limited to dark metal `[38–50, 42–50, 48–55]`, concrete `[175–190, 175–190, 165–185]`, muted civic red `[150–185, 45–70, 40–60]`, and small off-white accents `[205–230, 200–225, 185–210]`.
- Materials use `Metal`, `Concrete`, and at most one small `SmoothPlastic` accent; no glass, neon, chrome-bright colors, or photoreal micro-detail.
- Silhouettes remain chunky: no structural member is thinner than 0.12 studs and no decorative part is smaller than 0.10 studs in two axes.

## FireHydrant_Civic

- Total height is 3.0–3.6 studs; maximum body width is 1.5–2.1 studs.
- Base diameter/width is 0.45–0.60× total height and visibly exceeds the central barrel width.
- Central barrel occupies 0.45–0.60× total height.
- Inventory contains one base, one central barrel, one domed/capped crown, two opposed side nozzles, and one top nut.
- Side-nozzle center height is 0.45–0.65× total height; their combined span exceeds the barrel width by at least 25%.
- Uses muted civic red for the body and dark metal/off-white only for caps, nuts, or collar accents.
- Four-angle silhouette shows a stable base, readable crown, and two-sided nozzle crossbar.
- Anti-features absent: plain cylinder, needle-thin stem, floating nozzles, oversized top cap, or traffic-cone silhouette.

## TrashCan_Slatted

- Total height is 3.2–4.2 studs; width and depth are each 2.1–3.0 studs.
- Height-to-width ratio is 1.25–1.70.
- Inventory contains one stable base/plinth, 8–12 vertical perimeter slats, a top rim, and a recessed/dark opening.
- Slats occupy at least 65% of total height and have visible gaps of 0.10–0.30 studs.
- Top opening width/depth is 0.45–0.75× overall width/depth and sits below a rim at least 0.15 studs thick.
- Dark metal is the dominant palette; concrete may appear only as the base.
- Four-angle silhouette reads as a bin from every side and remains rotationally balanced.
- Anti-features absent: solid featureless box, mug handle, chimney stack, fully open bottom, or opening wider than the body.

## Bench_City

- Seat height is 1.4–1.8 studs; total height is 3.2–4.2 studs; overall width is 6.5–9.0 studs.
- Seat depth is 1.8–2.7 studs; width-to-depth ratio is 2.8–4.5.
- Inventory contains 3–5 seat slats, 3–5 back slats, two end supports/legs, and at least two seat-to-back braces.
- Back rises 1.5–2.5 studs above the seat and leans no more than 15° from vertical.
- Leg spacing is 0.55–0.80× overall width; each ground-contact foot is at least 0.35 studs wide.
- Dark metal frames the bench; slats use muted dark metal or a restrained brown within the brick palette.
- Four-angle silhouette clearly separates seat, back, and under-seat negative space.
- Anti-features absent: bed/table silhouette, solid slab back, center-only pedestal, floating slats, or armrests blocking the full seat.

## UtilityBox_Curbside

- Total height is 3.5–5.0 studs; width is 2.4–3.6 studs; depth is 1.5–2.4 studs.
- Height-to-width ratio is 1.20–1.75 and depth is 0.50–0.80× width.
- Inventory contains one concrete pad, one cabinet body, one inset front door, two visible hinges, one handle/latch, and 2–4 ventilation slots.
- Concrete pad extends 0.15–0.35 studs beyond the cabinet on every side and is 0.20–0.45 studs tall.
- Door inset is 0.03–0.12 studs from the front face; door width is 0.65–0.90× cabinet width.
- Dark desaturated metal is dominant, with concrete base and one small off-white warning plate permitted.
- Four-angle silhouette reads as a shallow curbside cabinet with a distinct front and stable pad.
- Anti-features absent: shipping-crate proportions, window-like opening, glowing panel, roof overhang, floating cabinet, or details protruding more than 0.25 studs.
